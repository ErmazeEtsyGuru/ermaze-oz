ERMAZE OS

ERMAZE OS is a private AI-assisted workflow system designed to help one Etsy seller manage original product creation, print-on-demand preparation, listing workflows, compliance review, pricing analysis, and store performance.

ERMAZE OS is not a public marketplace app and does not provide access to other Etsy sellers' accounts. It is intended solely for the owner's own Etsy shop.

About ERMAZE OS

ERMAZE OS coordinates specialized AI-assisted workflows for a single seller-owned Etsy shop. The system is designed to reduce repetitive administrative work while keeping product, compliance, profitability, and publishing decisions traceable.

What the application does

The system supports seller operations from early product research through draft listing preparation and post-launch analysis.

01

Original Product Design

Generates original product concepts and seller-directed artwork for digital and print-on-demand products. The workflow is designed to avoid copying other sellers' protected artwork.

02

Print-on-Demand Preparation

Prepares design assets, product templates, mockups, pricing inputs, and fulfillment data for connected print-on-demand services.

03

Etsy Listing Workflow

Assists with listing titles, descriptions, tags, pricing, images, draft creation, and updates for the owner's Etsy shop.

04

Compliance Review

Includes workflow checks intended to reduce intellectual-property, marketplace-policy, and listing-quality risks before a product is approved for publishing.

05

Profitability Analysis

Evaluates expected production costs, selling price, marketplace fees, and projected margin before products are prioritized for launch.

06

Performance Analysis

Reviews authorized shop performance data to help the seller understand which products and experiments are performing well and which should be revised.

Privacy & Data Use

ERMAZE OS is built for private use by the shop owner. Access to Etsy data occurs only after the account owner authorizes the application.

Data accessed

The application may access shop, listing, and performance information that is necessary to perform seller-authorized workflows. Access is limited to the permissions granted by the account owner.

Data sharing

ERMAZE OS is not designed to sell Etsy account data or provide Etsy account data to unrelated third parties. Data is used to support the owner's store-management workflow.

The seller remains responsible for reviewing products, listings, pricing, and compliance before enabling live automated publishing.
